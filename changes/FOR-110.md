# An agent may ask a person a question, and the answer goes back into its step

- `ask` is an outcome any agent role may declare (the shipped developer and the
  code and frontier reviewers do). The question is a record with the question,
  two or more options and a recommendation; a malformed one is an error outcome.
- The task waits as at a human gate, holds the queue, and shows in Needs you
  ("Answer question") and as the task page's decision, with the options, the
  recommendation marked and a field for an answer in words.
- Answer with `foreman task answer <task> --option <n> | --text <words>`, on the
  task page, or `POST /api/tasks/{id}/answer`. Who answered and when is recorded
  and the Activity shows both. The same step resumes its session with the answer
  as the next message, or runs again with it in its prompt; its visit count does
  not grow.
- The human gate keeps approve, deny and steer for now (see the manual).
